Workforce Deployment Case Study

Large-scale manpower supply delivered with full Qiwa, Muqeem and MLSD compliance across KSA

Large-Scale Workforce Deployment in Riyadh

Basmat Al Mawared mobilized 1,200+ background-checked blue-collar and white-collar personnel for a major Riyadh construction program. Every worker was onboarded through Qiwa, with Muqeem transfers, medicals, insurance and WPS payroll completed before site induction.

Deployment was staged across 6 weeks to match the client's phase ramp-up, achieving 100% attendance from day one and zero compliance issues in Ministry of Labor and Saudi Ministry audits.

Basmat Al Mawared staffed our entire project safely and on schedule. Their compliance team handled every Qiwa and Muqeem detail - we simply received workers ready to work. Fully 100% MLSD compliant.

Fahad Al-Otaibi

HR Director, Riyadh Contractor

For phase two, the client contracted our enterprise manpower supply agreement covering flexible staffing, payroll management and ongoing Nitaqat alignment - keeping their Saudization classification at green-tier while meeting aggressive delivery targets.

Our end-to-end model - from sourcing and vetting to deployment, transfers and end-of-service benefits - gave the client a single accountable partner for the full workforce lifecycle across the Kingdom.
