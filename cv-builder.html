    Why your CV format matters

    Applicant Tracking Systems read a CV as plain text before any human sees it. Anything that breaks that reading — a photo, a two-column layout, a logo, text inside a table or a CV saved as an image — can cause an otherwise strong candidate to be filtered out automatically.

    Every template here is single-column, uses standard section headings the software expects, and exports as selectable text rather than a picture of text. That is the difference between being read and being discarded.

    What the scanners reject
    • Photographs and personal graphics
    • Two-column and sidebar layouts
    • Text placed inside tables or boxes
    • Headers and footers holding contact details
    • Decorative fonts and icons
    • CVs exported as an image or scan
