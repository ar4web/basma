Navigating KSA Qiwa & Muqeem Transfers

  • Omar Al-Harbi
  • Jan 1, 2022
  • 12 Comments

Transferring employees between sponsors in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia is a routine yet highly regulated process. With the Qiwa platform now serving as the single digital gateway for labor services, employers must keep their records current to avoid penalties and service interruptions.

A Muqeem transfer is the practical step that follows approval on Qiwa: the employee's sponsor record is updated with the Ministry of Interior, the worker receives the updated residence permit (Iqama), and the new sponsor assumes full responsibility for the worker.

The fastest, most compliant way to onboard transferred staff is to hand the full process - Qiwa approvals, Muqeem records, medical, insurance and Iqama updates - to a licensed manpower partner.

Basmat Al Mawared's compliance desk manages every transfer end to end, achieving clearances in as little as a few business days. Because we maintain continuous records on Qiwa and Muqeem, transferred workers are deployable immediately upon completion, with zero gaps in legal status.

Why documentation accuracy matters

Inaccurate or delayed transfer records trigger Ministry of Labor warnings, affect your Nitaqat classification, and can halt the mobility of your entire workforce. Keeping a single accountable partner who processes every transfer correctly protects both your compliance rating and your project timelines.

How a manpower partner simplifies the process

From GOSI and medical bookings to insurance, WPS payroll and end-of-service benefits, a dedicated manpower supplier bundles every administrative step. Employers focus on operations while the partner guarantees 100% MLSD-compliant, background-checked personnel at every site.
