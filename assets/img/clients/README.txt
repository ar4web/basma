CLIENT LOGOS — PLACEHOLDER FILES
================================

All ten .png files here are INVENTED company names created for layout only.
They are deliberately fictional: Arkan, Najd, Tamkeen, Al Waha, Durrah,
Nukhba, Sahara, Anjal, Rawabi, Mizan.

No real company logo is used, and no real trademark appears.

REPLACING THEM
--------------
Drop your real client logos in this folder, then update the marquee list in
index.html (section id="clients"). Each logo appears TWICE in the markup —
once normally and once with aria-hidden="true". That duplication is what makes
the scroll loop seamlessly, so add every new logo to both halves of the track.

Recommended format:
  PNG with transparent background
  About 320 x 130 px, logo no taller than 62 px
  Under 20 KB each

The CSS renders logos in greyscale at 55% opacity and reveals full colour on
hover, so mixed logo colours still look consistent.

BEFORE YOU PUBLISH REAL LOGOS
-----------------------------
Check your contracts. Some Saudi clients, especially government entities,
banks and healthcare groups, prohibit suppliers from displaying their name
or logo publicly. Written permission is the safe route.

If a client cannot be named, leave them out. The heading above the marquee
says "more than 30 organisations", which carries the credibility without
naming anyone.
