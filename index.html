About Us

Operating since 2017. Trusted by more than 30 organisations for legal, compliant and rapid workforce deployment across the Kingdom.

We supply uniformed, background-checked personnel to every sector of the Saudi economy.

Basmat Al Mawared Company Limited is a licensed manpower supply company based in Riyadh. Since 2017 we have recruited, deployed and managed workforce for logistics, hospitality, cleaning, manufacturing and facilities clients across Saudi Arabia.

Every placement is made on a Qiwa contract with WPS salary payment, GOSI registration and medical insurance. Transfers run through Muqeem. We handle Nitaqat alignment so your operation stays in the green tier.

Clients keep one point of contact. We supply the people, the uniforms, the paperwork and a replacement if someone does not show. That is the whole offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about compliant manpower supply, worker transfers and deployment in Saudi Arabia.

Are your transfers fully Qiwa and Muqeem compliant?

Yes. Every worker we supply is registered on the Qiwa platform and processed through official Muqeem channels, with insurance, medical and contract documentation completed before deployment.

How quickly can you deploy personnel across the Kingdom?

Standard deployments are completed within 3-7 days depending on the role and location. For annual and enterprise agreements, we guarantee priority allocation with deployment in as little as 48 hours.

How do you support Saudization and Nitaqat requirements?

We recruit qualified Saudi nationals for supervisory and administrative roles, and structure your workforce mix to maintain a green-tier Nitaqat rating, keeping your organization fully aligned with Vision 2030.

What happens if a worker does not show up?

We provide a 48-hour guaranteed replacement under our annual and enterprise agreements. For monthly supply plans, replacement is prioritized at no additional cost.

Do you handle GOSI, medical insurance and WPS payroll?

Yes — all statutory obligations are included: GOSI registration, medical insurance, WPS-compliant salary processing, and end-of-service benefit calculations.

Can you source roles outside your listed categories?

Absolutely. Our listed categories cover the most common requirements, but we recruit across all MLSD-approved professions. If your role sits outside the list, we will source it.
