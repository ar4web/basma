Manpower Solutions Tailored to KSA

From single executive placements to multi-thousand-man labor deployments, Basmat Al Mawared delivers background-checked personnel, 100% MLSD compliant documentation, and rapid mobilization across the Kingdom.

End-to-End Workforce Supply & Compliance

Basmat Al Mawared Company Limited manages the complete workforce lifecycle - recruitment, visa processing, Qiwa registration, Muqeem transfers, medical and insurance, WPS payroll, and end-of-service benefits - so your operations run uninterrupted.

  • Background-checked personnel ready for rapid deployment across KSA.
  • Full Qiwa, Muqeem and GOSI processing handled by our compliance desk.
  • 100% MLSD compliant, with transparent pricing and SLAs.

Our recruitment team sources, vets and onboards candidates across construction, manufacturing, hospitality, oil & gas and white-collar sectors, aligned with Saudization and Nitaqat green-tier requirements.

Whether you need monthly flexible staffing, dedicated contract teams or an enterprise manpower supply agreement, we scale to your demand and guarantee compliant, reliable labor - with dedicated account management from Riyadh headquarters.
